MY CASES — INDEPENDENT WEBSITE (CLOUD)

This version runs on a paid online server, not on your laptop.
Your phone can access it from court using mobile data, even when your laptop is off.

1. Create a NEW PRIVATE GitHub repository.
2. Upload the CONTENTS of this CourtTrack_Cloud_Website folder (not the folder itself).
   IMPORTANT: render.yaml must be visible directly on the GitHub main branch.
3. On render.com, select New > Blueprint, connect the repository, Blueprint Path: render.yaml.
4. When Render prompts for COURTTRACK_PASSWORD, create a strong UNIQUE password (12+ chars).
   The session secret is generated for you. Review the paid compute/disk charges.
5. Deploy; when Live, open the HTTPS site on your phone, sign in, and use /mobile.

After deployment you do NOT need the PC to stay on.
Use the official High Court display board to verify hearing status.

Read README.md for details, limits and troubleshooting.
